| Normal titles | **Octarine** by Alexander Slobzheninov | `octarine/Octarine-Light.*`, `octarine/Octarine-Bold.*` | Light & Bold are free for personal and commercial use (check the readme that comes with the download) |
| Floating keywords | **Organo** by Nikola Nikolov | `organo/Organo.*` | Free (Creative Commons Attribution, credit the designer) |

Octarine and Organo aren't included yet. Drop the files into their folders with the names above
(`.woff2`, `.woff`, `.otf` or `.ttf` all work) and they switch on automatically. Until then the site falls back to the system font.
